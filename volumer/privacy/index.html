Volumer – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026 · Applies to the Volumer app for Android and iOS

In short

  • No account, no ads, no advertising ID. We never sell or share your data for advertising.
  • Your volume settings, profiles, automation rules, calendar, location and app usage stay on your phone.
  • Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports are sent only if you allow them. Both are off until you choose, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings → Privacy.
  • The app never records, listens to or analyses the sound you play. It only changes volume levels and audio effects.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of personal data processed through the app is Himdeve s. r. o., a company registered in the Slovak Republic (European Union) (“we”, “us”).

Contact for any privacy question or request: himdeve@gmail.com.

We have not appointed a data protection officer, because we are not required to. Because we are established in the EU, we have no separate EU representative; for the UK, Switzerland and other countries you can contact us directly at the address above.

3. Data sent only with your consent

On first launch the app asks whether it may send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. Until you answer “Allow”, nothing is collected for these purposes. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy; switching off stops further collection immediately.

3.1 Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

  • What: a random app-instance identifier generated by the app, the screens you open and features you use (for example “sleep timer started”), session start and length, app version, device model, operating system version, language, and approximate country or region derived from your IP address (the IP address itself is not stored by Google Analytics), and whether you are an “early adopter” of the app.
  • Not collected: advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS), identifier for vendor (iOS), your name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, precise location, the content of your calendar, the sound you play. Ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation signals are permanently denied.
  • Why: to understand which features are used and improve the app.
  • Retention: event data is kept for up to 14 months, then deleted automatically by Google.

3.2 Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

  • What: when the app crashes or hits a serious error: the technical error and stack trace, device model, operating system version, app version, free memory and disk space, device orientation, a random installation identifier, and short technical log lines from the moments before the error (these do not contain personal content).
  • Why: to find and fix bugs.
  • Retention: 90 days.

4. Data processed to run the app

These features work without an account and use only technical, pseudonymous data:

  • Remote settings (Firebase Remote Config and Firebase Installations). The app downloads settings from us (for example which features are free, or a short notice from the developer). For this, the app sends a random installation identifier, the app's identifier and version, the platform and language to Google's Firebase servers. Your IP address is necessarily visible to the server.
  • News notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging). If “News and tips” is on in Settings → Privacy, the app subscribes to a general news topic, which requires a push token for your device (on iOS through Apple Push Notification service). We send the same message to everyone; we never send personalised messages. Turn the switch off, or disable notifications in your phone's settings, to stop them. On iOS, these notifications arrive quietly in Notification Centre unless you allow more.
  • Developer notices with images. Notices in Settings may show an image loaded from a web server; that server sees your IP address when the image loads, like any website.
  • Purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. We never receive your payment details. The app receives from the store only which products you own and the purchase status, to unlock features.

5. Legal bases (EU / EEA, UK, Switzerland)

  • Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); and Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive for access to your device): usage statistics and crash reports. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): providing the app and features you purchased.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): delivering remote settings, keeping the app secure and working, and sending general news you can switch off. Our interest is operating and improving the app; we minimise the data involved and it is not used to identify you. You may object at any time (see section 9).
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): when the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

6. Service providers and international transfers

We use service providers (processors) who process data only on our instructions:

  • Google (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC) – Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Installations and Cloud Messaging. Firebase privacy information, Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play and Apple – app distribution, purchases and push delivery, as independent controllers under their own policies.

Some of these providers process data in the United States and other countries outside the EEA. Such transfers are based on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (Google LLC is certified), and/or Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, and their UK and Swiss equivalents.

We do not sell personal data, “share” it for cross-context behavioural advertising, or use it for targeted advertising or profiling that has legal or similarly significant effects.

7. How long we keep data

  • On-device data: until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Usage statistics: up to 14 months.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Installation identifiers and push tokens: until they are deleted. Settings → Privacy → Delete collected data deletes them together with your statistics; Google then removes them from its live and backup systems within 180 days.
  • E-mails you send us: as long as needed to answer you, and then up to 3 years in case of follow-up questions or legal claims.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). On-device data is stored in the app's private storage, protected by your phone's operating system; the protection PIN is stored only as a salted hash. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and limit what is collected in the first place.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy (portability);
  • have it corrected or deleted;
  • restrict or object to its processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time (Settings → Privacy);
  • not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing (we make none);
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection authority – in Slovakia the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic, or the authority where you live or work.

Good to know: we don't have your name, e-mail or any account, and the statistics and crash identifiers are random and not linked to you, so we cannot look up “your” data ourselves. That is why the app lets you act directly: Settings → Privacy → Delete collected data deletes the identifiers and the statistics linked to them at Google (removed from Google's systems within 180 days) and unsent crash reports, and gives the app new random identifiers. You can also switch off statistics, crash reports and news there, or uninstall the app, which deletes all on-device data. You can also write to us; we answer within one month (in the US within 45 days), and may ask for information needed to verify the request.

11. Other countries

We follow the same principles everywhere. If you live in, for example, Brazil (LGPD), Canada (PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25), the United Kingdom (UK GDPR), Switzerland (nFADP), Australia (Privacy Act), Japan (APPI), South Korea (PIPA), India (DPDP Act), South Africa (POPIA) or another country with privacy laws, you have the rights given to you by those laws, and you can exercise them by writing to us. Where those laws call for it, the legal bases and transfer safeguards in sections 5 and 6 apply equally.

12. Children

The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country, up to 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Statistics and crash reports are off unless allowed. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
